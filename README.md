# Don Ferruccio

Landing page for **Don Ferruccio** — a restaurant proposal in **Gijón, Asturias, Spain**.

GitHub Pages site serving as a coming-soon page while the business plan takes shape.

## Concept

Italian cuisine with a dual revenue model:
- **35%** in-person dining (locale in Gijón)
- **65%** delivery

## Team

- **Graham** (Scotland) — tech & operations
- **Charlize** (Uruguay-born, Italian family, raised in Argentina) — culinary & concept

Met in a car park in Asturias. The rest is unwritten.

## Style Direction

70s/80s retro patch aesthetic — inspired by Italian Dolomites mountaineering patches,
football club badges, and South American flair. Retro warmth blended with a modern,
futuristic edge.
